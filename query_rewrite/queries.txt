SFU Health & Counselling Services offer a range of resources and support services to students, including health and well-being outreach events/programs, qualitative evaluations, and support services in collaboration with other university departments. To access consultations at SFU, students can visit the student health clinics on Burnaby and Vancouver campuses, book appointments with healthcare professionals, and explore available resources and support options. History: I feel suicidal
If you or someone you know is feeling suicidal, it's essential to reach out for help immediately. You can call the 24/7 crisis line at 1-800-784-2433 (1-800-SUICIDE) or contact your local crisis center. For support within the SFU community, you can reach out to the following campus numbers: 
- Burnaby campus: 778.782.4615
- Vancouver campus: 778.782.5200
- Surrey campus: 778.782.5200

Additionally, you can provide immediate support by learning to recognize signs of distress, asking about suicidal thoughts, connecting individuals to services, and offering a caring and empathetic atmosphere. It's important to attend workshops, take online courses, contact Registered Clinical Counsellors, and support mental health initiatives like the Hi-FIVE Movement for Mental Health Program at SFU. For more information on suicide prevention resources and immediate support options, please visit the following URLs: [SFU Suicide Prevention Workshops](https://www.sfu.ca/students/health/get-support/support-groups/groups/suicide-prevention.html#course) and [SFU Crisis Support](https://www.sfu.ca/students/health/get-support/talk-now.html). Remember, help is available, and you are not alone.
