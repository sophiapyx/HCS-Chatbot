Question: How can I provide immediate support to someone at risk of suicide?
Answer: - Learn how to recognize potential signs of distress and suicide risk
- Ask about suicidal thoughts
- Effectively respond to students in distress using interpersonal skills
- Connect individuals to SFU and community-based services
- Attend the Suicide Prevention and Awareness Workshop
- Take the Online Course
- Contact Registered Clinical Counsellors for support 
- Offer a caring and empathetic atmosphere
- Learn about stigma reduction
- Support the Hi-FIVE Movement for Mental Health Program at SFU

Question: How can I clarify what help looks like for someone struggling with thoughts of suicide?
Answer: - Assure people that services are confidential
- Encourage them to reach out to mental health professionals
- Offer resources and referrals as needed
- Acknowledge the student's choice to take the referral
- Reinforce that taking that step may help them reach positive change
